Our Mission

The Bay Area Cyber Spartans is a community-driven movement dedicated to peak physical performance. We provide the opportunity to train with a team that pushes you to your absolute limits.

From the annual Murph Challenge to local Spartan Races, we believe that fitness is better when done together.
